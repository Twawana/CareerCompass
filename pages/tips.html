Job Search Strategies

Resume/CV Tips

  • Create a professional CV
    Use Canva or free online templates to design a clean, modern resume that highlights your skills
  • Tailor your CV for each application
    Adjust keywords and emphasize relevant experience for each job you apply to
  • Include measurable achievements
    Use numbers and statistics to show your impact (e.g., "Increased sales by 20%")
  • Keep it concise
    Limit your resume to 1-2 pages maximum, focusing on recent and relevant experience

Application Advice

  • Apply even if you don't meet all requirements
    Many job descriptions list ideal qualifications - apply if you meet 60-70% of them
  • Leverage your network
    Ask family, friends, or former colleagues if they know of any job opportunities
  • Follow up after applying
    Send a polite email 1-2 weeks after submitting your application to express continued interest
  • Apply early
    Submit applications within the first 3 days of posting when possible

Skill Development

Online Learning

  • Take free online courses
    Platforms like Coursera, edX, and Khan Academy offer free courses you can add to your CV
  • Learn in-demand skills
    Focus on skills like basic coding, digital marketing, or data analysis that are valuable across industries
  • Build a portfolio
    Create sample projects or case studies to demonstrate your skills to employers
  • Get certified
    Earn free or low-cost certifications from Google, Microsoft, or HubSpot to boost your credentials

Volunteer & Experience

  • Gain experience through volunteering
    Local organizations often need help with tasks that can build your skills and resume
  • Start freelancing
    Offer services on platforms like Fiverr or Upwork to build real-world experience
  • Document all experience
    Include informal work (helping family businesses, personal projects) on your resume

Interview Success

Preparation

  • Practice common questions
    Rehearse answers to "Tell me about yourself" and "Why should we hire you?"
  • Research the company
    Understand their mission, products, and recent news before the interview
  • Prepare questions to ask
    Have 2-3 thoughtful questions ready about the role and company culture
  • Dress appropriately
    When in doubt, dress slightly more formally than the company's everyday attire

During the Interview

  • Focus on confidence
    Clear communication and enthusiasm often matter more than perfect English
  • Use the STAR method
    Structure answers with Situation, Task, Action, Result when discussing experiences
  • Show enthusiasm
    Express genuine interest in the role and company throughout the conversation
  • Bring copies of your resume
    Have several printed copies available, even for virtual interviews

Quick Start Tips

  • Start small - Even $5/day adds up to $150/month
  • Combine methods - Do 2-3 things simultaneously for better results
  • Track your time - Focus on activities with the best payoff for your effort
  • Reinvest earnings - Use early income to fund better tools/equipment

Maximizing Your Earnings

  • Start with what you have - Use existing skills and equipment before investing money
  • Combine methods - Do surveys while waiting for delivery requests
  • Track everything - Use a simple spreadsheet to monitor what earns most per hour
  • Scale what works - Focus more time on your most profitable activities
  • Automate where possible - Use tools to handle repetitive tasks
